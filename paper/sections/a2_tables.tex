Table~\ref{tab:e4runs} lists the twelve training runs behind the cell means
of Table~\ref{tab:e4cells} and the effects of Table~\ref{tab:e4effects}.
Both derived tables are computed from these rows by the repository's own
analysis module, and the rows are given here so that the derivation can be
checked without the repository.

The measurement files behind the other three experiments are wider than a
page and are not reproduced here. Table~\ref{tab:e1} is the complete
resolution sweep; Tables~\ref{tab:e2} and~\ref{tab:e3} report the
sample size and condition used in the body, and the remaining sample sizes
and conditions are in the committed files named in the
reproducibility statement.
